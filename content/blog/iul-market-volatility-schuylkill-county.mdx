## Key Concepts

An **Indexed Universal Life (IUL)** policy is a permanent life insurance plan that combines tax-free death protection with a cash value accumulation vehicle linked to major market indices like the S&P 500.

### The Power of the 0% Floor

The most distinctive advantage of an IUL is the **0% Floor Guarantee**. When market indices perform well, your cash value earns positive interest credited up to a cap rate. But if the market experiences a severe downturn, your cash value is credited with 0% interest—meaning **you never lose a single penny of your accumulated principal or previously locked-in gains due to market crashes**.

### Tax-Free Income in Retirement

When you contribute to a 401(k) or traditional IRA, you defer taxes until retirement. When you finally take withdrawals, every dollar is taxed as ordinary income—potentially at higher future tax rates. With an IUL, your cash value grows tax-deferred, and you can access your accumulated funds through tax-free policy loans during retirement, creating a **tax-free income stream** that won't trigger higher income brackets or affect Social Security taxation.

### Permanent Protection & Legacy Transfer

Unlike temporary term insurance, an IUL stays in place for your entire life. It guarantees a tax-free cash death benefit for your surviving family or estate, giving you dual benefits: wealth accumulation while you live and income protection for your family after you pass.

<Callout type="tip">
Think of an IUL as a financial safety vault with a one-way ratchet: as market indices climb, your gains are permanently locked in; when the market drops, your balance remains completely protected from loss.
</Callout>
