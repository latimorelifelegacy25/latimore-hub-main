## Key Concepts

An **Indexed Universal Life (IUL)** policy is a permanent life insurance contract that combines permanent coverage with a flexible cash value account linked to major stock market indices (such as the S&P 500).

### 1. Capital Protection via the 0% Floor Guarantee
For self-employed business owners, protecting liquid capital is critical. Unlike traditional stock portfolios that can drop 20% to 30% during economic downturns, an IUL contains a contractual **0% downside floor guarantee**. When the market index rises, your cash value earns interest up to an annual cap; when the market crashes, **your account earns 0% interest, ensuring you never lose accumulated principal or locked-in gains to market volatility**.

### 2. Tax-Free Cash Liquidity for Business Needs
Under IRS Code Section 72(e) and Section 101(a), cash value inside an IUL grows tax-deferred and can be accessed **100% tax-free** via policy loans. Local business owners can tap into this cash reserve to:
*   Purchase new equipment, commercial vehicles, or inventory.
*   Fund business expansion or bridge seasonal cash flow gaps.
*   Provide key-person coverage or buyout funding for business partners.
*   Create a tax-free supplemental income stream for personal retirement.

### 3. Integrated Living Benefits Protection
If a critical illness (such as a heart attack, stroke, or cancer) prevents you from managing your business daily, built-in Living Benefit riders allow you to accelerate a portion of your policy's death benefit as direct cash—giving you the financial runway needed to hire key staff or keep payroll running while you recover.
