## The Question That Keeps Retirees Up at Night

"What if I outlive my money?"

It's one of the most common concerns we hear from families across the coal region, and it's a legitimate one. You spend decades working, saving, and building a life — and then you're handed a lump sum, a 401(k) balance, or a pension statement and told to make it last for however long you happen to live. Nobody hands you the answer key.

An annuity is one of the few financial tools built to solve exactly that problem. In simple terms, it's a contract with an insurance company that converts savings into a personal paycheck for life — income that keeps arriving whether you live to 75 or 95, and regardless of what the stock market is doing that year.

## How Annuities Complement What You Already Have

Most Schuylkill County retirees are relying on a combination of Social Security and, for many, a pension. Both are valuable, but they were never designed to cover every expense on their own — and pensions in particular are becoming less common with each new generation of retirees.

An annuity fills the gap between what Social Security and pension income provide and what your actual monthly expenses require. Rather than drawing down a retirement account and hoping it lasts, retirees can convert a portion of their savings into a guaranteed income stream that arrives on the same schedule as their other checks, for as long as they live.

* **Predictability:** A fixed or fixed indexed annuity's income payments don't shrink when the market drops.
* **Longevity protection:** Payments are structured to continue for life, so a longer-than-expected retirement doesn't become a financial crisis.
* **Tax-deferred growth:** Money inside an annuity grows without annual tax drag until it's withdrawn.

## A Legacy of Planning Ahead

There's something distinct about how Schuylkill County families approach retirement. From Pottsville to Tamaqua, Shenandoah to Mahanoy City and Frackville, generations here have shown up, done the work, and made sure the people they loved were taken care of. That spirit didn't fade when the mines closed — it lives on in how local families still plan ahead and still believe a better tomorrow is worth working toward today.

Annuities fit naturally into that legacy. They're not about chasing the highest possible return; they're about certainty — knowing the income will be there no matter what happens in the wider economy.
