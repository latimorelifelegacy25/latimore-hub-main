The "top barrier" preventing Americans from buying life insurance is the persistent myth that it is a luxury expense. When we let a "wild guess" dictate our financial strategy, we engage in a form of unintentional self-sabotage. We talk ourselves out of essential protections before we even see a quote.

In reality, modern underwriting has made coverage more accessible and affordable than most realize. By replacing myths with math, Schuylkill County families often discover they can secure their future for less than the cost of a monthly streaming subscription. ## 2. The "Living Benefit" You Can Use for Your Will (and College)

Insurance shouldn't just care for your family when you're gone; it should help you build a legacy while you are still here. Because fraternal benefit societies like Foresters have no shareholders to satisfy, the money that would typically be paid out as profit is instead reinvested into unique member benefits.

Eligible members can use [Foresters LawAssure](https://www.foresters.com/en/member-benefits/lawassure) to help with estate-planning documents. This online service allows you to create customized wills, powers of attorney, and even living trusts and pet trusts—critical documents that ensure your wishes are respected.

Beyond legal protection, these benefits offer immediate financial support for your family's growth:

- **Competitive Scholarships:** Education is a cornerstone of any legacy. Eligible member families can apply for [Foresters Competitive Scholarships](https://www.foresters.com/en/member-benefits/scholarships); award amounts and renewal requirements depend on the applicable program year. This is a game-changer for local students looking to attend Penn State Schuylkill, Alvernia University, or other regional programs.
- **Community Grants:** For those driven by a "human-first" mission, Foresters offers [community grants](https://www.foresters.com/en/member-benefits/community-grants) to eligible members, subject to program requirements. This includes grants for organizing community clean-ups or supporting local youth initiatives like Schuylkill Community Action's Summer BLAST program.

## 3. A New Lifeline for Schuylkill County Residents Living with Diabetes

A diabetes diagnosis can affect life insurance eligibility and premiums. [Foresters explains](https://www.foresters.com/en/well-being/empowering-healthy-living/life-insurance-for-people-with-diabetes-2) that health history, diabetes management, and A1C levels affect underwriting. Schuylkill County residents should review their individual options rather than assume that coverage is unavailable.

Foresters Live Well Plus training materials include a diabetes screening question about whether the last HbA1c reading exceeded 10.9%. This is one screening criterion, not a promise of approval; medication use, complications, age, test timing, and the full underwriting review also matter. Source: [Foresters Live Well Plus training materials (2025)](https://ezbiz.foresters.com/foresters-livewell-plus-training-ppt).

Many of these options utilize a "Non-Medical" underwriting path. While insurability still depends on answers to medical questions and an underwriting review, this path often requires no blood tests, fluids, or invasive exams. It is a streamlined approach that respects the dignity and time of those managing chronic conditions.

## 4. Your Legacy Includes a Check for Your Favorite Local Charity

Most people want to be remembered for the causes they championed, but few feel they have the disposable wealth to be a "philanthropist." The Charity Benefit Provision turns everyday policyholders into "accidental philanthropists."

When a claim is paid, Foresters pays an additional 1% of the face amount (up to a maximum of $100,000) to a designated 501(c)(3) organization of the owner's choice. This is an additional payment under eligible certificates that does not reduce the beneficiary's payout. Source: [Foresters Strong Foundation product terms (accessed October 11, 2026)](https://www.foresters.com/en/life-insurance/term-life-insurance/strong-foundation-term).

For residents of Schuylkill County, this means you can support local staples like the Schuylkill Area Community Foundation (SACF), the Schuylkill United Way, or Schuylkill Community Action as a final act of service—without taking a single cent away from your family's inheritance.

## 5. Protection for the Storm: Natural Disaster Coverage

Modern life insurance is pivoting to help families survive immediate environmental catastrophes. While Schuylkill County might not see typhoons or tsunamis, severe Pennsylvania storms, flooding, and tornadoes are very real threats to local homes and safety. The Family Health Benefit Rider is an automatically included feature that recognizes the reality of our changing climate.

If an insured person or an immediate family member requires medical attention due to a qualified natural disaster (like a tornado, hurricane, or being struck by lightning), this rider provides specific cash benefits to help manage the chaos. The coverage includes:

- **Ambulance Transportation:** $50. Source: [Foresters Live Well Plus training materials (2025)](https://ezbiz.foresters.com/foresters-livewell-plus-training-ppt).
- **Emergency Room Examination:** $100. Source: [Foresters Live Well Plus training materials (2025)](https://ezbiz.foresters.com/foresters-livewell-plus-training-ppt).
- **Hospital Stay:** $100 per day (for up to 5 days). Source: [Foresters Live Well Plus training materials (2025)](https://ezbiz.foresters.com/foresters-livewell-plus-training-ppt).

The rider has a $5,000 lifetime family maximum and a $650 per-person, per-incident maximum, subject to qualifying-event definitions and certificate terms. It may help with immediate medical costs following a covered disaster. Source: [Foresters Live Well Plus training materials (2025)](https://ezbiz.foresters.com/foresters-livewell-plus-training-ppt).
