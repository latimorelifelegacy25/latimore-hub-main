When we let a "wild guess" dictate our financial strategy, we engage in a form of unintentional self-sabotage. We talk ourselves out of essential protections before we even see a quote.

In reality, modern underwriting has made coverage more accessible and affordable than most realize. By replacing myths with math, Schuylkill County families often discover they can secure their future for less than the cost of a monthly streaming subscription. Mark Rush, Chief Distribution Officer at Foresters, views this work through a lens of community impact rather than just sales:

> "Your work doesn't just help protect families, it helps shape a brighter future."

Shaping that future requires us to stop guessing and start planning.

## 2. The "Living Benefit" You Can Use for Your Will (and College)

Insurance shouldn't just care for your family when you're gone; it should help you build a legacy while you are still here. Because fraternal benefit societies like Foresters have no shareholders to satisfy, the money that would typically be paid out as profit is instead reinvested into unique member benefits.

Many Americans do not have a will, and Foresters policyholders may be able to use LawAssure to bridge that gap. This online service allows you to create customized wills, powers of attorney, and even living trusts and pet trusts—critical documents that ensure your wishes are respected.

Beyond legal protection, these benefits offer immediate financial support for your family's growth:

- **Competitive Scholarships:** Education is a cornerstone of any legacy. Foresters awards renewable tuition scholarships each year; eligibility and award amounts are set by the program (see [Foresters member benefits](https://www.foresters.com/en/member-benefits), reviewed October 2026). This is a game-changer for local students looking to attend Penn State Schuylkill, Alvernia University, or other regional programs.
- **Community Grants:** For those driven by a "human-first" mission, Foresters funds community grants each year through its community programs (see [Foresters member benefits](https://www.foresters.com/en/member-benefits)); amounts and eligibility are set by the program. This includes grants for organizing community clean-ups or supporting local youth initiatives like Schuylkill Community Action's Summer BLAST program.

## 3. A New Lifeline for Residents Living with Diabetes

For decades, a diagnosis of Type 1 or Type 2 diabetes was often a "hard no" from insurance carriers. Today, the landscape has shifted. Diabetes is a common health concern across Pennsylvania, including the Coal Region, which makes accessible coverage important.

Through products like Strong Foundation and Live Well Plus, Foresters has introduced groundbreaking updates that remove the traditional "fear of the needle" or rejection. In a move that challenges old industry norms, individuals with elevated HbA1c readings may be eligible for coverage under certain Foresters underwriting guidelines. Qualifying thresholds are set by the carrier and change over time, so confirm current guidelines with a licensed agent. This applies to both Type 1 and Type 2 diabetes, providing a vital lifeline to a demographic that has been historically underserved.

Many of these options utilize a "Non-Medical" underwriting path. While insurability still depends on answers to medical questions and an underwriting review, this path often requires no blood tests, fluids, or invasive exams. It is a streamlined approach that respects the dignity and time of those managing chronic conditions.

## 4. Your Legacy Includes a Check for Your Favorite Local Charity

Most people want to be remembered for the causes they championed, but few feel they have the disposable wealth to be a "philanthropist." The Charity Benefit Provision turns everyday policyholders into "accidental philanthropists."

When a claim is paid, Foresters' Charity Benefit Provision may pay an additional amount to a qualifying charity chosen by the owner, subject to the policy's terms and limits. Review the policy contract for the exact amount. This is not a redistribution of funds; it is an additional gift from the insurer that does not reduce the beneficiary's payout.

For residents of Schuylkill County, this means you can support local staples like the Schuylkill Area Community Foundation (SACF), the Schuylkill United Way, or Schuylkill Community Action as a final act of service—without taking a single cent away from your family's inheritance.

## 5. Protection for the Storm: Natural Disaster Coverage

Modern life insurance is pivoting to help families survive immediate environmental catastrophes. While Schuylkill County might not see typhoons or tsunamis, severe Pennsylvania storms, flooding, and tornadoes are very real threats to local homes and safety. Depending on the product, a family health benefit rider may be included that recognizes the reality of our changing climate.

If an insured person or an immediate family member requires medical attention due to a qualified natural disaster (like a tornado, hurricane, or being struck by lightning), a rider like this may provide limited cash benefits toward ambulance transportation, emergency room visits, or hospital stays. Benefit amounts, lifetime limits, and exclusions vary by product and are listed in the policy contract, so review them before relying on this feature.
