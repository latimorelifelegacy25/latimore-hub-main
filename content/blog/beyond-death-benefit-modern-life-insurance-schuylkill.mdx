## 2. The "Living Benefit" You Can Use for Your Will (and College)

Insurance shouldn't just care for your family when you're gone; it should help you build a legacy while you are still here. Because fraternal benefit societies like Foresters have no shareholders to satisfy, the money that would typically be paid out as profit is instead reinvested into unique member benefits.

While 76% of Americans currently lack a will, policyholders can use LawAssure to bridge that gap. This online service allows you to create customized wills, powers of attorney, and even living trusts and pet trusts—critical documents that ensure your wishes are respected.

Beyond legal protection, these benefits offer immediate financial support for your family's growth:

- **Competitive Scholarships:** Education is a cornerstone of any legacy. Member families can apply for hundreds of tuition scholarships worth up to $2,500 annually, with the ability to reapply for up to three additional years. This is a game-changer for local students looking to attend Penn State Schuylkill, Alvernia University, or other regional programs.
- **Community Grants:** For those driven by a "human-first" mission, Foresters provides up to $3,400 annually to support local volunteerism and immediate needs. This includes grants for organizing community clean-ups or supporting local youth initiatives like Schuylkill Community Action's Summer BLAST program.

## 3. A New Lifeline for the 9% of Schuylkill County Living with Diabetes

For decades, a diagnosis of Type 1 or Type 2 diabetes was often a "hard no" from insurance carriers. Today, the landscape has shifted. In Schuylkill County, where an estimated 9% of adults have been told they have diabetes, this is a critical issue. The county currently ranks among the least healthy in Pennsylvania for overall health outcomes, making accessible coverage even more essential.

Through products like Strong Foundation and Live Well Plus, Foresters has introduced groundbreaking updates that remove the traditional "fear of the needle" or rejection. In a move that challenges old industry norms, individuals with HbA1c readings as high as 10.9% may now qualify for coverage. This applies to both Type 1 and Type 2 diabetes, providing a vital lifeline to a demographic that has been historically underserved.

Many of these options utilize a "Non-Medical" underwriting path. While insurability still depends on answers to medical questions and an underwriting review, this path often requires no blood tests, fluids, or invasive exams. It is a streamlined approach that respects the dignity and time of those managing chronic conditions.

## 4. Your Legacy Includes a Check for Your Favorite Local Charity

Most people want to be remembered for the causes they championed, but few feel they have the disposable wealth to be a "philanthropist." The Charity Benefit Provision turns everyday policyholders into "accidental philanthropists."

When a claim is paid, Foresters pays an additional 1% of the face amount (up to a maximum of $100,000) to a designated 501(c)(3) organization of the owner's choice. This is not a redistribution of funds; it is an additional gift from the insurer that does not reduce the beneficiary's payout.

For residents of Schuylkill County, this means you can support local staples like the Schuylkill Area Community Foundation (SACF), the Schuylkill United Way, or Schuylkill Community Action as a final act of service—without taking a single cent away from your family's inheritance.

## 5. Protection for the Storm: Natural Disaster Coverage

Modern life insurance is pivoting to help families survive immediate environmental catastrophes. While Schuylkill County might not see typhoons or tsunamis, severe Pennsylvania storms, flooding, and tornadoes are very real threats to local homes and safety. The Family Health Benefit Rider is an automatically included feature that recognizes the reality of our changing climate.

If an insured person or an immediate family member requires medical attention due to a qualified natural disaster (like a tornado, hurricane, or being struck by lightning), this rider provides specific cash benefits to help manage the chaos. The coverage includes:

- **Ambulance Transportation:** $50
- **Emergency Room Examination:** $100
- **Hospital Stay:** $100 per day (for up to 5 days)

With a $5,000 lifetime family limit, this feature acts as a vital buffer during the first 72 hours of recovery, helping families navigate the immediate costs of a disaster.
