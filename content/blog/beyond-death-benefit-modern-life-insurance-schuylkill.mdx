## 2. The "Living Benefit" You Can Use for Your Will (and College)

Insurance shouldn't just care for your family when you're gone; it should help you build a legacy while you are still here. Some fraternal benefit societies offer eligible members additional services or programs alongside insurance. These are separate from accelerated death benefit riders and are not available with every policy.

Some membership programs offer access to online estate-planning document tools, which may include wills, powers of attorney, and trusts. Availability and eligibility vary, and complex needs should be reviewed with a qualified attorney.

Beyond legal protection, these benefits offer immediate financial support for your family's growth:

- **Competitive Scholarships:** Some membership programs offer scholarship opportunities for eligible families. Award amounts, selection criteria, and renewal requirements vary. Local students should review eligibility alongside other education-funding options.
- **Community Grants:** Some membership programs offer grants for eligible volunteer projects, such as community clean-ups or local youth initiatives. Funding is subject to program requirements and approval.

## 3. A New Lifeline for Schuylkill County Residents Living with Diabetes

A diabetes diagnosis can affect life insurance eligibility and premiums. Diabetes management and A1C levels can affect underwriting. Schuylkill County residents should review their individual options rather than assume that coverage is unavailable.

Underwriting considers the full health history. Medication use, complications, age, test timing, and other factors may affect eligibility; no single screening result promises approval.

Many of these options utilize a "Non-Medical" underwriting path. While insurability still depends on answers to medical questions and an underwriting review, this path often requires no blood tests, fluids, or invasive exams. It is a streamlined approach that respects the dignity and time of those managing chronic conditions.

<Callout type="info">
Living with diabetes does not necessarily rule out life insurance. Eligibility depends on the product and the full underwriting review. Review current guidelines with your agent.
</Callout>

## 4. Your Legacy Includes a Check for Your Favorite Local Charity

Most people want to be remembered for the causes they championed, but few feel they have the disposable wealth to be a "philanthropist." A charitable giving feature, where available, may help policyholders include an eligible charitable organization in their legacy plan.

Some eligible policies provide an additional charitable payment when a covered claim is paid. Amounts, eligible organizations, and whether the payment affects the family’s benefit depend on the contract. Latimore Life & Legacy can help you review those terms.

For residents of Schuylkill County, this means you can support local staples like the Schuylkill Area Community Foundation (SACF), the Schuylkill United Way, or Schuylkill Community Action as a final act of service, subject to the policy’s charitable giving terms.

## 5. Protection for the Storm: Natural Disaster Coverage

Modern life insurance is pivoting to help families survive immediate environmental catastrophes. While Schuylkill County might not see typhoons or tsunamis, severe Pennsylvania storms, flooding, and tornadoes are very real threats to local homes and safety. Depending on the product, a family health benefit rider may be included that recognizes the reality of our changing climate.

Some policies may offer a supplemental rider for medical expenses following a covered natural disaster. Covered events, eligible family members, payment amounts, exclusions, and benefit limits vary by contract. Review the actual rider rather than assuming this protection is included.
