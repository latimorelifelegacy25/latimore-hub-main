## Introduction

Life in Schuylkill County is defined by hard work, deep roots, and strong communities. From the factories in Pottsville to the local businesses keeping Tamaqua and Shenandoah moving, our residents know the value of an honest dollar. But what happens if a sudden medical crisis takes you out of the workforce?

Many families rely on standard life insurance to protect their loved ones after they are gone. While that is a critical piece of the puzzle, a traditional policy doesn't help you if you survive a severe illness but are left unable to work. That's where **Term Living Benefits** come in—allowing you to access a portion of your policy's death benefit while you are still alive to cover everyday expenses when you need it most.

## The Reality of Living Expenses in the Skook

We live in a resilient region, but a serious medical event can quickly upend a household budget. According to recent federal and local data, the economic landscape in Schuylkill County requires careful planning:

* **Household Budgets:** The U.S. Census Bureau reports the median household income in Schuylkill County sits at **$68,313**.
* **Fixed Obligations:** Data from *doxoInsights* shows that the average local household spends roughly **$1,453 per month** just on essential bills and housing.

If a primary breadwinner suffers a heart attack, stroke, or severe injury on the job, those bills don't stop. Traditional life insurance only pays out at death, leaving a massive financial gap during recovery. Term life with living benefits allows you to advance a portion of your death benefit early if you are diagnosed with a qualifying critical, chronic, or terminal illness.
